6-pack fundamentals
🔥
Fat loss is done in the kitchen
You cannot out-train a bad diet. Abs are built by eating at a deficit — consistently.
💪
Protein is non-negotiable
High protein preserves muscle during fat loss. Hit your daily target every single day.
😴
Sleep 7–8 hours
Poor sleep spikes cortisol, breaks muscle, and increases cravings. No shortcut here.
🚶
Walk 8,000–10,000 steps daily
NEAT (non-exercise activity) burns more fat over time than gym sessions alone.
📅
Consistency beats perfection
One bad day won't ruin progress. Missing two weeks will. Show up every day.
